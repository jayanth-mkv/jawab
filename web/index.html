The voice agent that learns from every call it can’t answer.

Jawab answers calls for India’s small businesses in Hindi and English, using only facts the owner has confirmed. When it doesn’t know, it doesn’t guess: it asks the owner by voice, calls the customer back, and publishes the answer to the web — then verifies it went live.

1 · Customer callsGemini Live answers in Hindi-English from confirmed facts.
2 · Gap detectedUnknown? It holds, takes callback consent, reports the gap.
3 · Owner speaksA 10-second Hindi voice note becomes a structured fact.
4 · Customer toldMid-call, or the customer’s phone rings with the callback.
5 · On the webPublished as FAQ + FAQPage structured data, after approval.
6 · VerifiedThe live page is re-checked; AI-search visibility is probed.
